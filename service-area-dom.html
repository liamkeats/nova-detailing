FAQ

Questions people usually ask before booking

Do you come to my house?

Yes. Most of our mobile detailing appointments are done at homes, as long as there is enough space to work safely around the vehicle.

Can you detail at my workplace?

Usually, yes. Workplace appointments can work well if the parking area allows it, we have permission to work there, and the service setup makes sense.

Can you detail in an apartment parking lot?

Sometimes. Apartment lots can work if there is enough space, property permission, and access to water or power if the service requires it.

Do you need water or power?

For most mobile appointments, access to water and power is preferred and may be required depending on the service. We confirm the setup before booking.

Do you charge travel fees?

Nearby appointments are usually included. Farther appointments may have a small travel fee depending on distance, schedule, package, and number of vehicles.

What if I am outside your normal area?

You should still ask. We do not automatically reject farther addresses if the job size, schedule, service type, or number of vehicles makes the trip worthwhile.

Do you travel farther for ceramic coating or paint correction?

Yes. Bigger services like ceramic coating and paint correction are more likely to make extended travel worthwhile.

Do you offer fleet detailing?

Yes. We may be able to help with company vehicles, work trucks, dealership vehicles, and multiple vehicles at the same location.
